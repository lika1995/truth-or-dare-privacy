Privacy Policy

Truth or Dare by LikLilly Games

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LikLilly Games ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Truth or Dare mobile game (the "Game"). It applies to the current Android release of the Game.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game stores information locally on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs and other local app storage where applicable. This may include player names and selected gameplay preferences, user-created challenges, language and sound settings, other Game settings, and your analytics consent status.

This information is used to provide the Game and remember your choices. LikLilly Games does not receive this locally stored gameplay information merely because it is saved on your device. It remains on your device until you change or delete it, clear the Game's app data, or uninstall the Game.

Your device operating system or account provider may include the Game's local data in system or cloud backups. Those backups are controlled by the relevant platform and your device or account settings, and they may remain after local app data is cleared or the Game is uninstalled.

2. AppMetrica analytics: current status and future consent-based use

The Game project contains an AppMetrica integration, but it is currently inactive. As of the date of this policy, AppMetrica analytics is not activated and is not sending analytics statistics. The description below explains the intended behavior only if analytics is activated in a future Game build.

If activated, analytics will operate only after the player gives voluntary consent. Refusing analytics will not limit the Game's functionality. The player will be able to change this choice at any time in Settings.

After consent, AppMetrica may process Game launches, sessions, technical information about the device and operating system, application or installation identifiers, device identifiers used for analytics, and the device's IP address. This data will be processed by the AppMetrica service operated by Yandex to help us understand and improve the Game.

Withdrawing consent will disable further transmission of analytics statistics. Data transmitted before consent was withdrawn, or data previously accumulated locally, may remain subject to the applicable retention and deletion mechanisms.

We do not plan to use advertising identifiers, precise geolocation, automatic purchase data collection, or AppMetrica crash reporting. For more information, see AppMetrica privacy and data security information and the Yandex Privacy Policy.

3. Unity diagnostics and build-specific configuration

Unity Engine Diagnostics is disabled in the current local Unity project configuration intended for a future build. This local configuration does not prove or guarantee that the first AAB already uploaded to Google Play, or any other previously built or distributed version, was created with diagnostics disabled.

The diagnostics behavior of an installed version depends on the configuration included in that specific build. Until an earlier build is independently verified, its diagnostics state should not be inferred from the current local project configuration.

4. In-app purchases

The Game uses Google Play Billing for in-app purchases. Purchase and payment processing is handled by Google Play. Google may process information such as your Google account information, device information, transaction details, product identifier, purchase status, and purchase token under Google's own policies.

The Game may receive purchase status, product identifiers, and purchase tokens from Google Play on your device as needed to complete, acknowledge, or restore a purchase. LikLilly Games does not receive or store your full payment card details.

For more information, see the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

5. Ratings and reviews

The Game may display the Google Play In-App Review flow. If you choose to submit a rating or review, your rating and any text you enter are sent to Google Play. Google may publish the review on the Play Store or, for a closed testing track, share it privately with the developer. The Game does not separately collect the text of your review.

6. How information is shared

We do not sell personal information. Google processes information when you choose to use Google Play services described above, such as purchases or reviews, under Google's policies. If AppMetrica analytics is activated in a future build and you consent, the analytics data described in Section 2 will be processed by AppMetrica (Yandex). We may also disclose information if required by law.

7. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the Game. If you believe that a child under 13 has sent personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and delete it where applicable.

8. Your choices and data deletion

  • You can change available Game preferences and your analytics consent choice in the Game's Settings.
  • If analytics is activated in a future build, withdrawing consent will stop further transmission of analytics statistics, but it may not delete data already transmitted or previously accumulated locally.
  • You can remove locally stored Game data by clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling the Game. System or cloud backups may be retained by the relevant platform under its own policies and settings.
  • You can manage or delete Google Play ratings and reviews through your Google Play or Google Account.
  • For purchase information and other data processed by Google, use the controls and requests described in Google's privacy policy.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date.

10. Contact

If you have questions or requests about this Privacy Policy or privacy in the Game, contact LikLilly Games at likusya865@gmail.com.
