Privacy Policy

Truth or Dare by LikLilly Games

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LikLilly Games ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Truth or Dare mobile game (the "Game"). It applies to the Android version of the Game. The data processing described below may depend on the features and SDK configuration included in the installed version.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game stores information locally on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs and other local app storage where applicable. This may include player names and selected gameplay preferences, user-created challenges, language and sound settings, other Game settings, and your analytics consent status.

This information is used to provide the Game and remember your choices. LikLilly Games does not receive this locally stored gameplay information merely because it is saved on your device. It remains on your device until you change or delete it, clear the Game's app data, or uninstall the Game.

Your device operating system or account provider may include the Game's local data in system or cloud backups. Those backups are controlled by the relevant platform and your device or account settings, and they may remain after local app data is cleared or the Game is uninstalled.

2. AppMetrica analytics and consent

The Game includes AppMetrica analytics integration. In versions where analytics is enabled, AppMetrica is activated only after the player provides voluntary consent.

Refusing analytics will not limit the Game's functionality. Players can change their analytics consent choice at any time in Settings.

After consent, AppMetrica may process Game launches, sessions, technical information about the device and operating system, application or installation identifiers, device identifiers used for analytics, and the device's IP address. Approximate geographic information, such as the country, may be derived from the IP address.

This data is processed by AppMetrica, operated by Yandex, to help us understand Game usage and improve the Game.

Withdrawing consent disables further analytics transmission by the Game. Data previously transmitted or accumulated locally may remain subject to applicable retention and deletion mechanisms.

The Game's configured AppMetrica integration does not enable advertising identifier collection, precise location tracking, automatic purchase tracking, or AppMetrica crash reporting.

For more information, see AppMetrica privacy and data security information and the Yandex Privacy Policy.

3. Unity diagnostics and build-specific configuration

Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, and Unity Cloud Diagnostics are disabled in the current Unity project configuration.

The configuration of a particular installed version depends on the settings included when that version was built. Previously distributed builds may have different configurations.

Other services, including Google Play Billing, may process technical and diagnostic information necessary for their operation.

4. In-app purchases

The Game uses Google Play Billing for in-app purchases and subscriptions. Purchase and payment processing is handled by Google Play.

Google may process information such as your Google account information, device information, transaction details, product identifiers, purchase status, purchase tokens, and technical diagnostics under Google's own policies.

The Game may receive purchase status, product identifiers, and purchase tokens from Google Play on your device as needed to complete, acknowledge, verify, or restore a purchase and determine access to subscription features.

LikLilly Games does not receive or store your full payment card details.

For more information, see the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

5. Ratings and reviews

The Game may display the Google Play In-App Review flow.

If you choose to submit a rating or review, your rating and any text you enter are sent to Google Play.

Google may publish the review on the Play Store or, for a closed testing track, share it privately with the developer. The Game does not separately collect the text of your review.

6. How information is shared

We do not sell personal information.

Google processes information when you use Google Play services described above, including purchases and reviews, under Google's applicable policies.

When AppMetrica analytics is activated with your consent, the analytics data described in Section 2 is transmitted to and processed by AppMetrica (Yandex) as our analytics service provider.

We may also disclose information if required by applicable law.

7. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for users aged 16 and older and is not directed to children under 16.

We do not knowingly request personal contact information from children through the Game.

The Game does not require users to create an account. Any analytics processing is subject to the consent mechanism described in Section 2.

If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about information relating to a minor, please contact us using the details below.

8. Your choices and data deletion

  • You can change available Game preferences and your analytics consent choice in the Game's Settings.
  • Withdrawing analytics consent stops further analytics transmission by the Game in versions where AppMetrica is enabled, but does not automatically delete data previously transmitted.
  • You can remove locally stored Game data by clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling the Game. System or cloud backups may be retained by the relevant platform under its own policies and settings.
  • You can manage or delete Google Play ratings and reviews through your Google Play or Google Account.
  • For purchase information and other data processed by Google, use the controls and requests described in Google's privacy policy.
  • For questions or requests concerning analytics information, you may contact us using the address below. We will review requests in accordance with applicable law and the capabilities of the relevant service provider.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date.

10. Contact

If you have questions or requests about this Privacy Policy or privacy in the Game, contact LikLilly Games at likusya865@gmail.com.
